# OpenApply beta participation record

Keep this completed record in an owner-only location. Do not commit it to the
repository or attach it to a public issue. This template records the user's
choices; it is not a substitute for the product privacy and site-terms review.

- Participant ID (use an internal alias): `{{ID}}`
- Date and time of consent: `{{DATE_TIME}}`
- Operator/contact for stopping a run: `{{CONTACT}}`
- Operating system and app version: `{{OS_AND_VERSION}}`
- Approved role families and exclusions: `{{ROLES_AND_EXCLUSIONS}}`
- Approved providers and employer sites: `{{PROVIDERS_AND_SITES}}`
- Daily application cap: `{{CAP}}`
- Final submission policy (automatic for supported forms or ask each time): `{{POLICY}}`
- Browser account/session use approved: `{{YES_OR_NO}}`
- Optional mailbox code access approved, with provider and scopes: `{{DETAILS_OR_NO}}`
- Résumé tailoring from verified work history approved: `{{YES_OR_NO}}`
- Monitoring data and retention period explained and accepted: `{{YES_OR_NO}}`
- Export/deletion and withdrawal method explained: `{{YES_OR_NO}}`
- Candidate confirmation of choices: `{{SIGNATURE_OR_EQUIVALENT}}`

The participant can pause or withdraw by contacting the operator. A withdrawn
participant's scheduled runs stop before another application is attempted.
