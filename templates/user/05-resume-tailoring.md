# Résumé Tailoring Rules

## Required format

- Template file: `{{PATH TO LATEX OR DOCX TEMPLATE}}`
- Maximum pages: `{{ONE OR TWO}}`
- Preserve colors, typography, spacing, and section structure: `{{YES/NO}}`
- Required sections: `{{SUMMARY, SKILLS, EXPERIENCE, PROJECTS, EDUCATION, CERTIFICATIONS}}`

## Per-job workflow

1. Read the complete official job description.
2. Extract the title, company, prioritized responsibilities, hard requirements, repeated keywords, salary, location, and work arrangement.
3. Compare every requirement with verified candidate claims.
4. Write a truthful headline matching the role family.
5. Write a three-to-four-sentence summary using only verified experience.
6. Reorder experience and project bullets by relevance without changing chronology.
7. Select 8–12 supported skills using the job posting's wording where accurate.
8. Compile to PDF, render every page to images, and visually inspect the result.
9. Upload only the verified role-specific PDF.

## Bullet formula

Use a result-first X–Y–Z structure when the evidence supports it:

- X: the accomplishment or outcome.
- Y: the verified measurement or scope.
- Z: the action, method, or tool that produced it.

Examples:

- With a verified metric: `{{RESULT}} by `{{ACTION}}`, affecting `{{VERIFIED SCALE}}`.
- Without a metric: `{{IMPACT BY SCOPE OR RISK}}` by `{{ACTION}}` using `{{TOOLS}}`.

## Prohibited behavior

- Do not redesign the approved template during tailoring.
- Do not add keywords unsupported by experience.
- Do not hide hard requirement gaps.
- Do not claim business outcomes that were not measured.
- Do not upload the master résumé when a role-specific version is required.

## Visual QA checklist

- No clipping, overlap, or broken characters.
- No orphaned headings or mostly empty trailing pages.
- Consistent margins, dates, bullet indentation, and hyperlink styling.
- Text remains selectable and ATS-readable.
- Final filename includes company and role without personal secrets.
