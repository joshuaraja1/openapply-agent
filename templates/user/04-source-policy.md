# Claim Source Policy

## Precedence

Rank sources from most to least authoritative.

1. `{{CURRENT WORK-HISTORY SOURCE OF TRUTH}}`
2. `{{CURRENT MASTER RESUME}}`
3. `{{PORTFOLIO}}`
4. `{{GITHUB}}`
5. `{{USER-CONFIRMED APPLICATION ANSWERS}}`
6. `{{OTHER SOURCES}}`

When two sources conflict, use the higher-ranked source and quarantine the conflicting claim.

## Allowed source uses

- Work-history file: matching, résumé, applications, interviews.
- Master résumé: formatting reference and corroboration; `{{ALLOW OR DISALLOW CLAIM RESTORATION}}`.
- Portfolio: `{{ALLOWED USES}}`.
- GitHub: `{{ALLOWED USES}}`.
- LinkedIn profile: `{{PROFILE URL ONLY / ALLOWED USES}}`.

## Naming restrictions

- Employers that may be named: `{{LIST}}`
- Clients that may be named: `{{LIST OR NONE}}`
- Generic client descriptions to use: `{{EXAMPLES}}`
- Confidential systems, metrics, or customer names: `{{RESTRICTIONS}}`

## Truthfulness rules

- Never invent jobs, dates, metrics, tools, degrees, certifications, clients, or qualifications.
- Never convert part-time, internship, contract, or project work into full-time employment.
- Use a number only when a source verifies it.
- When no number exists, show impact through verified scope, complexity, audience, or risk.
- Do not infer a demographic, disability, veteran status, security clearance, or criminal-history answer.
- Treat a missing sensitive answer as a stop condition, not permission to guess.
