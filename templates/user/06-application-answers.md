## Stop and ask

Never guess these answers:

- GPA, SAT, ACT, licenses, clearances, or dates not in the source of truth.
- Criminal-history or government-contracting disclosures.
- Conflicts of interest or outside-employment commitments.
- Export-control, citizenship, passport, or sanctions questions beyond the approved facts.
- Any legal attestation the user has not reviewed.
